Privacy policy

Refract SEO Chrome extension · Effective 14 October 2026 (updated for data sources, Search Console and Bing)

Short version: Refract SEO has no account, no server and no analytics. It reads a page only when you open it on that page. Results stay in your browser. A few optional features contact public services, listed below with exactly what each one receives.

What Refract SEO reads

  • The page you audit: when you click the toolbar icon (or have the side panel or full-screen view open for a tab), Refract SEO reads that tab's HTML, headings, links, images, meta tags, structured data, performance timings and HTTP response headers. Nothing is read from other tabs.
  • The site's own public files: robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemaps and, if the page declares one on the same site, its canonical URL. These are fetched from the page itself.
  • Google search results pages: on google.*/search Refract SEO numbers results and can extract "People also ask" questions. This happens locally in the page; nothing about your searches is sent anywhere. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Refract SEO does not read form inputs, passwords, cookies or browsing history.

Chrome built-in AI

AI suggestions use Gemini Nano, which runs on your device. Page text, headings and image files given to the model never leave your computer and are not sent to Refract SEO or to Google by Refract SEO. Suggestions are checked by Refract SEO before they are shown, and any suggestion that states a number not found on the page is discarded.

What Refract SEO never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or crash reporting.
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of data to third parties beyond the requests listed above.
  • No remote code: everything Refract SEO runs ships inside the extension.
  • No use of data for advertising, credit or lending decisions.

Your control

Remove Refract SEO from chrome://extensions to delete its stored settings and crawl results. You can revoke the optional "all sites" access there too (Details → Site access). Turning off Google results features is in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the new version ships inside the extension.
